&#x20;\*\*Aurora Atlas\*\*



\### 1. Theme Name

\*\*Aurora Atlas\*\* — Premium, aspirational travel with soft atmospheric depth.



\### 2. Design Philosophy

\*"Travel should feel magical before you pack."\*  

Inspired by Apple Travel, Apple Maps, and premium iOS apps. Calm confidence, generous whitespace, photography-forward hero moments. AI feels like a thoughtful concierge, not a chatbot.



\### 3. Target Audience

Couples and luxury-leaning travelers (25–40) who value aesthetics and trust polished products.



\### 4. Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |

|------|-------|-----|

| Primary | Deep Ocean | `#0F3D5E` |

| Secondary | Mist Blue | `#7BA7BC` |

| Accent | Aurora Coral | `#FF6B5B` |

| Background | Cloud White | `#FAFBFC` |

| Surface | Soft Gray | `#F2F4F7` |

| Text | Charcoal | `#1A1D23` |

| Text Muted | Slate | `#6B7280` |



\### 5. Typography

\- \*\*Headings:\*\* `SF Pro Display` / `Inter` (600–700, tight tracking on H1)

\- \*\*Body:\*\* `Inter` or `SF Pro Text` (400–500, 16px base, 1.6 line-height)

\- \*\*Data/ costs:\*\* `Inter` tabular nums, medium weight



\### 6. Layout Structure

\- \*\*12-column grid\*\*, max-width 1200px

\- Sticky top nav (logo left, auth right)

\- Itinerary: \*\*vertical timeline\*\* left rail (days) + scrollable content right

\- Cost summary: \*\*floating sticky panel\*\* on desktop (right), bottom sheet on mobile



\### 7. Card Design

\- Large-radius cards (16–20px)

\- Subtle shadow: `0 4px 24px rgba(15,61,94,0.08)`

\- Hero destination cards with \*\*full-bleed image\*\* + gradient overlay

\- Activity cards: image thumbnail left, metadata right

\- No heavy borders; separation via shadow and spacing



\### 8. Buttons \& Form Elements

\- \*\*Primary:\*\* Filled deep ocean, white text, 12px radius, 44px min height

\- \*\*Secondary:\*\* Ghost with 1px border `#E5E7EB`

\- \*\*Generate CTA:\*\* Accent coral with subtle gradient

\- Inputs: Large (48px), soft gray fill, focus ring in secondary blue

\- Travel style: \*\*Pill selectors\*\* with icon + label, single-select



\### 9. Animations \& Micro-interactions

\- Page transitions: 200ms ease fade + 8px upward slide

\- Generation: \*\*aurora shimmer\*\* progress bar + pulsing dot constellation

\- Day switch: crossfade content, timeline dot scales 1→1.2

\- Save: checkmark draw animation + haptic-style bounce

\- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`



\### 10. Icon Style

\- \*\*SF Symbols–style\*\* line icons, 1.5px stroke, rounded caps

\- Duotone for category headers (attractions, food, hotels)



\### 11. Illustration Style

\- Soft \*\*3D gradient blobs\*\* and abstract map contours on empty states

\- No cartoon characters; optional subtle topographic line art



\### 12. Landing Page Design

\- Full-viewport hero: cinematic destination photo + headline \*"Your journey, intelligently planned"\*

\- Below: 3-step flow (Tell us → AI builds → Save \& go)

\- \*\*Live sample itinerary\*\* carousel (static demo, no sign-in)

\- Social proof strip: "Built for modern travelers"

\- Footer: minimal links, disclaimer



\### 13. Dashboard Design

\- \*\*My Trips:\*\* Masonry-style cards with destination photo, overlay title, days + budget badge

\- Empty state: illustration + "Plan your first voyage"

\- Top bar: greeting + "New Trip" accent button

\- No sidebar; clean top-nav IA



\### 14. Mobile Responsiveness

\- Bottom tab bar: Home | Trips | Profile

\- Itinerary day selector: \*\*horizontal scroll chips\*\* (sticky)

\- Cost panel collapses to expandable \*\*"Budget summary"\*\* bar

\- Thumb-zone CTAs fixed at bottom during generation flow



\### 15. Why This Fits an AI Travel Planner

Aspirational tone sells the \*dream\* of travel while structured timeline + sticky budget panel keeps AI output scannable. Feels trustworthy and premium — users believe the AI "curated" something special.
